Light Inferno

Level Design – Shooting Experience

A game level set inside a tower inspired by Dante’s Inferno, expanding progressively as the player ascends. Lights, colors, and dark spaces are designed to evoke discomfort and tension, while moving luminous elements intuitively guide the player and make the gameplay more dynamic. The sound design evolves with the player’s progression: each floor reached adds new instruments to the soundtrack, gradually intensifying rhythm and immersion. Targets, initially distant or unreachable, become accessible only by advancing through the level.
